    First-timer follow-up guide

    Hannah’s conversation guide: reconnect, learn what’s stopping the client from returning, then agree the right next step. Keep it natural and adapt to their answer.

    Before offering a First-Timer top-up, confirm eligibility, the amount payable and remaining credits in the client’s account.

    Endgame: six possible outcomesA useful next step can be more than a sale.

    Purchase

    Client buys their first package.

    Rebook

    Client confirms their next class.

    Renew

    Client buys another package as their current one ends.

    Upgrade

    Client moves from singles or a smaller pack to a suitable larger pack.

    Follow-up

    Client agrees to a specific time and way to reconnect.

    Recover

    Address a poor experience or lapse and agree a path back toward regular visits.

    Record what actually happened and why: the barrier, agreed action, outcome and contact preference. An offer is not a purchase; a suggested class is not a confirmed rebooking. Note a return separately when a client reactivates.
